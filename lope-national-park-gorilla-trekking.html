Ancient Petroglyphs & Cultural Heritage

Beyond its wildlife wonders, Lopé preserves exceptional archaeological treasures. Ancient stone tools and open-air rock petroglyphs carved into ironstone ridges date back over 400,000 years.

The park protects an unbroken record of human settlement and migration along the Ogooué Valley spanning from the Early Stone Age through the Iron Age.
